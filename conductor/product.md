# Product Definition: Turnos App

## Initial Concept
A scheduling application ("turnos-app") built with Next.js and Supabase, allowing users to create profiles, define event types, and manage bookings.

## Target Audience
- **Service Providers:** Professionals offering appointments (consultants, doctors, etc.).
- **Clients/Bookers:** End-users who need to book time slots with providers.
- **Business Owners:** Teams or small businesses needing shared scheduling.

## Core Features
- **Profile & Event Management:** Users can create public profiles and define different event types (duration, description).
- **Advanced Scheduling:** Tools for providers to manage complex availability and multiple appointment types.
- **Booking Flow:** A streamlined, mobile-first experience for clients to find and book available slots.
- **Calendar Sync:** Integration with external calendars (Google, Outlook) to prevent double bookings.
- **Notifications:** Automated email/SMS reminders for upcoming appointments to reduce no-shows.
- **Custom Branding:** Providers can customize their booking pages with colors and logos.

## Key Success Metrics
- **Booking Ease:** Minimize the number of steps and time required for a client to complete a booking.
- **User Onboarding:** A high percentage of providers successfully setting up their full profiles and event types.
- **Platform Stability:** Ensuring reliable performance and accurate scheduling under high concurrent loads.

## Design Vision
- **Minimalist UI:** A clean, focused interface that prioritizes the scheduling task.
- **Mobile-First UX:** A seamless experience on smartphones, as most clients will book on the go.
- **Customizable Identity:** Empowering providers to maintain their brand identity within the platform.
