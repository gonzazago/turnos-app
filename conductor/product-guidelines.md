# Product Guidelines: Turnos App

## Prose Style
- **Professional & Concise:** Our communication is direct and efficient. We prioritize clarity and brevity to help users complete their tasks without unnecessary distraction.

## Branding & Visual Identity
- **Modern & Sleek:** The visual design uses clean lines, ample white space, and a contemporary aesthetic to create a sense of order and professionalism.
- **Vibrant & Dynamic:** We use energetic accent colors and dynamic elements to create an engaging and lively experience for both providers and bookers.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Intuitive Flow:** The booking process is designed to be seamless and requires minimal cognitive load. Every step is clearly defined and logically follows the previous one.
- **Accessibility-First:** We are committed to making Turnos App usable by everyone. We follow WCAG standards to ensure a wide range of users can interact with our platform effectively.
- **Performance-Driven:** Speed is a feature. We optimize for fast load times and snappy interactions to respect our users' time and maintain a high level of engagement.

## Content Strategy
- **Action-Oriented:** We prioritize quick actions and minimize the amount of reading required. Information is structured to lead users directly to their next task or goal.
